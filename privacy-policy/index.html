By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Scope — Which Apps This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy covers all HutApp LLC applications. Our apps differ substantially in what they do, so the sections below describe the maximum set of information any HutApp app may process. A given app processes only what its own features require: where a section names a specific service, identifier, or permission, it applies only to the apps that actually use it.

Where this section and a general section below differ, this section governs.

Book Hut (iOS, watchOS)

Book Hut does not collect any data. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising software, requests no advertising identifier, and has no user account, subscription, or in-app purchase. No information is transmitted to HutApp LLC by this app under any circumstance. Sections 1, 3, 5, 6, 10, and 12 below do not apply to Book Hut.

Book Hut connects only to services you choose:

  • Your own media server. If you add an Audiobookshelf or Jellyfin server, the app sends the username and password you enter to that server in order to obtain an access token. Your password is not stored. The token, your library details, and your listening position are kept on your device, and your listening position is sent back to your own server so that your progress follows you between devices. Jellyfin servers additionally receive a randomly generated installation identifier, which the Jellyfin protocol uses to tell devices apart; it is not derived from your hardware and is not shared with anyone else.
  • LibriVox and the Internet Archive. If you browse the public-domain audiobook catalogue, your search terms and IP address reach librivox.org and archive.org, which are operated independently of HutApp LLC and under their own privacy policies.
  • Your Apple Watch. If you pair an Apple Watch, the app copies your server's access token and the certificate you approved for that server onto the watch, so the watch can download books from your server by itself when your iPhone is not with you. The copy passes directly between your two devices and is never sent to HutApp LLC or anyone else. Removing a server on your iPhone removes its copy from the watch.

Everything else — your books, bookmarks and any notes you write on them, your listening history and statistics, and your settings — stays on your device and in your device's own backup. Some features read your own books on the device to do their work: Book Hut can transcribe an audiobook you have downloaded so it can line it up with the ebook, and can write a short recap of what you have already heard. Both run entirely on your device, and no audio, transcript, or book text is sent to HutApp LLC or to any other service. iOS may download its own speech model the first time you use a language; that is a system download and carries none of your information. To erase everything, delete the app.

Book Hut requests one permission, on the iPhone and on the Apple Watch: local network access, so that each can reach a media server running on your own network. It requests no other permission.
